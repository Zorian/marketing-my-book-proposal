Steps 03 & 04
iv.
Routes & Platform

The delivery.

Two audiences. Two distinct paths to reach them. One platform that holds it all together.

— Audience 01
Professionals
Publicly accessible — they find you through search, LinkedIn, and industry channels.

Reachable through standard channels. They have budgets, decision power, and clear professional intent.

— Audience 02
Volunteers
They need to be mined. A newsletter and magazine — Big Issue in style, the opposite of Forbes.

A publication that meets them where they are, not where they aspire to be. Built to capture attention, build trust, and convert at their pace.

— Step 04 · The Platform

A website that turns attention into revenue.

The platform sits at the centre. Every audience, every channel, every product points back to it. It engages, captures, and converts — and it gives us the data to do all of this better, every quarter, for three years.
